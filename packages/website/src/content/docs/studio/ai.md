Describe the Template you need and let Copilot write the first draft, or ask it to propose improvements to the Template you're working on. Nothing changes until you accept it, and nothing is saved until you save.

## Before you start

- **GitHub Copilot:** an active Copilot subscription, signed in on this computer. Copilot is not a Connection Profile, and Studio stores no Copilot token. See [how the Copilot Session works](/connections/profiles/#ai-drafting-copilot-session).
- **Project Grounding (optional):** a [Connection Profile](/connections/profiles/#atomize-studio) chosen as the Work Project, with the [Project and Team (Read) scope](/connections/#required-permissions). Without one you can still draft and improve ungrounded.
- **Wiki pages (optional):** the optional **Wiki (Read)** scope on the same token. Local Markdown and text files need no connection.

You choose the Grounding for every run. Copilot receives your description or focus, the Template being improved, the Work Project's names and allowed values, and the Reference Documents you leave ticked. It never receives your token, Stories, or other work items.
