## Keep your work

Switching Studio Areas or using Back preserves your in-memory authoring session. Export or save to keep a file on disk; navigation history and companion-process recovery are not a saved backup. Read [Open, save, and export](/studio/open-save/) for the available save actions.

When you are ready to connect, follow the [Auth Guide](https://github.com/Simao-Pereira-Gomes/Atomize/blob/main/docs/Auth-Guide.md#quick-setup) and the [Studio Generate Area guide](https://github.com/Simao-Pereira-Gomes/Atomize/blob/main/docs/Atomize-Studio.md#generate-area).
